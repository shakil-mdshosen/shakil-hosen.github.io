Teleprompter controls

  • Play / PauseStart or stop auto-scrolling. Settings tuck away automatically while playing.
  • ResetReturn to the beginning of the script and clear the timer.
  • MirrorFlip text horizontally for use with teleprompter glass.
  • Guide lineShow or hide a reading guide across the middle of the stage.
  • FullscreenEnter or exit fullscreen mode.
  • Speed & font sizeAdjust scrolling speed (1–10) and text size (16–72px). Use the mouse wheel over either slider for fine control.
  • RecordStart or stop voice recording. The audio downloads automatically when stopped.
  • Stage coloursCustomise background and text colours. Your choice is remembered.
  • Settings toggleShow or hide the settings panel to give the stage more room.

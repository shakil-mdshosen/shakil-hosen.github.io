          How it works
        

      

      
    
        
  • Each line is trimmed and validated as IPv4 or IPv6; invalid lines are skipped and listed so you can fix them.
    • 
        
  • IPs are sent to proxycheck.io in batches of 100 (a short pause between batches), with VPN, ASN and risk detection enabled.
    • 
        
  • An IP is flagged when any of Proxy, VPN, Compromised or Hosting is true.
    • 
        
  • Everything runs in your browser. The addresses go straight to proxycheck.io; this site stores nothing.
